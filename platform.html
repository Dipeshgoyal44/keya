Platform Overview

The HybridAI platform is purpose built for enterprise customers, underpinned by the four key capabilities of Prediction, Optimization, Recommendation, and Orchestration.

Predict with Confidence

  • Illuminate future workloads and anticipate infrastructure demands.
  • Smart foresight unlocked — HybridAI maps, predicts, and optimizes your infrastructure.

Optimize with Precision

  • Dynamic resource allocation adapts to evolving workloads.
  • Cut waste, energy use, and total cost of ownership.

Recommend with Intelligence

  • Recommends the right AI infrastructure for each model and workload.
  • Accounts for compute intensity, latency sensitivity, and power limits.

Orchestrate with Speed

  • Automate deployment across K8s, OpenShift, VMware, on-premise and cloud.
  • From months to minutes, with validation built in.
